Our Firm

Who we are

Owen Hodge Lawyers is a top tier law firm that Sydney residents rely on. We commenced providing legal services to the St George and Sutherland communities in 1951. Since then, Owen Hodge Lawyers has grown considerably — developing a reputation for quality legal services, value for money and a strong commitment to excellence.

Today, the Owen Hodge Sydney lawyers provide expert advice in a diverse range of business legal services and personal law matters. We have Sydney offices in the city CBD, Hurstville and Turramurra.

We endeavour to keep our clients up to date with any changes in the law which might affect their business or private affairs. With our eBooks, videos and blog, you can access all of the latest information.

At all times, clients can feel confident Owen Hodge Lawyers will handle all matters with professionalism and efficiency. This is guaranteed with our Law 9000: Legal Best Practice certification. This is a benchmark management system based on internationally recognised standards and a set of best-practice criteria specific to a legal practice.

Est. 1951

Our history

Owen Hodge Lawyers was founded by Owen Hodge himself in 1951. Originally he traded under the name Owen D Hodge Solicitors later changing to Owen Hodge & Son Solicitors in 1974 to mark the partnership between Owen and his son Russell.

Owen undertook a great deal of work for Legacy despite not being a legatee himself, his mother was President of the local branch of Legacy. Owen was unique for his time in that he spent 6 years working overseas gaining experience and furthering his knowledge. He served for 3 years in the Australian Army and another 3 years as a navigator flying in Lancaster Bombers

Russell began working as a solicitor under Owen in July 1973 before being made a partner of the firm in July 1974. Owen was diagnosed with Motor Neuron Disease in 1980 and retired from practice in approximately 1984. Sadly he passed away in 1987.

Owen Hodge Lawyers provide expert legal services to a diverse range of individual and commercial law clients in areas ranging from and including Business Succession, Commercial Litigation, Commercial Property, Employment Law, Family Law, Wills & Estate Planning and Personal Injury. Our Hurstville, Sydney CBD and Oran Park Lawyers are highly experienced solicitors and attorneys.

We believe in being different. Our passion is to provide our clients with clarity, direction and security.
