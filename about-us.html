Our Story

Serving Sydney's communities since 1951

Owen Hodge Lawyers was established with a clear purpose: to provide high-quality legal services with professionalism, integrity and genuine care for our clients. Since 1951, we have grown from a small local practice into one of Sydney's most respected law firms — while never losing sight of what matters most.

We serve individuals, families and businesses across Sydney from our offices in Turramurra, Sydney CBD and Hurstville. Whatever the legal matter, our approach remains the same: listen carefully, advise clearly and advocate strongly.

Our team of experienced lawyers brings deep expertise across commercial law, family law, wills and estates, property law, immigration law and personal injury — ensuring our clients always have access to the right specialist for their needs.
