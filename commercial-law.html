Commercial Lawyers Sydney – Trusted Legal Advice for Businesses Across NSW

For any business, having the right commercial lawyers can be the difference between closing a successful deal and facing costly legal setbacks. Whether you're navigating a complex contract, dealing with an employment issue, or involved in litigation, expert legal guidance is essential to protect your rights and drive growth.

At Owen Hodge Lawyers, our experienced commercial lawyers in Sydney and Hurstville provide strategic legal advice tailored to your business needs. As one of the most respected commercial law firms in NSW, we're proud to support many of Sydney's most successful businesses – from startups to established enterprises.

Frequently asked questions

01
How does Owen Hodge Lawyers handle commercial disputes?
+
The approach to commercial disputes varies depending on the nature of the case. Our lawyers begin with a thorough assessment of the dispute and explore all available resolution options — including negotiation, mediation, and arbitration — before proceeding to litigation. Where court action is necessary, our experienced commercial litigators will represent your interests at every stage.
02
Can Owen Hodge Lawyers assist with corporate compliance issues?
+
Yes, Owen Hodge Lawyers are experienced in providing legal advice for corporate compliance issues. We help businesses understand their obligations under Australian law, implement compliant governance frameworks, and respond to regulatory inquiries. Whether you're a start-up or an established company, we provide practical guidance to keep your operations on the right side of the law.
03
Why Choose Our Business and Commercial Lawyers?
+
  • Accredited and experienced commercial law team
  • Offices in Sydney CBD and Hurstville for your convenience
  • Dedicated legal support for companies of all sizes
  • Cost-effective, practical legal solutions
  • Clear advice, delivered with a personable and proactive approach
04
What areas of commercial law do you cover?
+

Our corporate and business lawyers advise on a broad range of matters, including:

  • Commercial contracts and agreements
  • Business sales and acquisitions (M&A)
  • Commercial property leases and retail leasing
  • Corporate governance and compliance
  • Employment law and workplace relations
  • Business structuring and asset protection
  • Intellectual property advice
  • Debt recovery and commercial disputes
  • Business litigation and dispute resolution

Whether you're negotiating a new deal or responding to a legal challenge, our commercial lawyers are here to ensure your interests are protected and your business can operate with confidence.
